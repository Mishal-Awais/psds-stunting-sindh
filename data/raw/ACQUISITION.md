## Data Acquisition Record

The Pakistan Demographic and Health Survey (PDHS) 2017–18 was implemented by the National Institute of Population Studies (NIPS) under the Ministry of National Health Services, Regulations and Coordination. ICF provided technical assistance through The DHS Program, while the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics assisted with sample selection and household listing. The survey aimed to provide reliable information on population and health indicators, including fertility, maternal and child health, and nutritional status. The dataset was obtained from the official DHS Program website: https://dhsprogram.com/data/dataset/Pakistan_Standard-DHS_2017.cfm?flag=1. https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/SR257/SR257.pdf

I acquired the data by direct download on 12 September 2026. The downloaded archive is named PK_2017-18_DHS_09122026_535_258773.zip, and the file I identified for analysis is PKKR71FL.DTA, the Children's Recode file. Direct download was suitable because the provider distributes the survey files through its official website. I did not use an API, web scraping, or manual data collection because I obtained an existing dataset rather than collecting new observations. I will check these alternatives against the four acquisition methods taught in the course before finalising this paragraph.

The dataset is subject to The DHS Program's conditions of use. Users must follow the applicable terms and must not share the downloaded datasets with other researchers without written consent from The DHS Program. The raw microdata will therefore remain outside the public GitHub repository. I will keep the official source URL and document the file used for analysis. https://www.dhsprogram.com/data/model-datasets.cfm

A researcher may be able to obtain the dataset again from the official source, but reproducing the acquisition may require registration, an approved data request, or renewed acceptance of the applicable terms. The process could be affected if access requirements, the website, or the availability of the dataset changes. Reproducing the analysis could also fail if the original file is unavailable or a different file version is obtained. Recording the source URL, filename, access conditions, and analysis steps will make the process easier to audit.
